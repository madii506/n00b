01How it works

1. Sign in
Sign in

One free signature from your wallet. It is not a transaction and cannot move anything.

2. Enter
Hold to enter

Hold 0.01% of a coin and its room opens for you. Any Solana coin has one.

3. Talk
Talk with your a/s/l

Every line carries your wallet's age, size and location. Wallets under 7 days show up as n00b.

4. Kicked
Sell half, you're out

Drop to half your peak bag and the room kicks you, in front of everyone.

example.txt - reading a line
[21:04]n00b<fresh.one>0d0.03%20+gm, is this the room?
n00b

The wallet is younger than 7 days.

0d

Age. Time since the wallet's first transaction on Solana.

0.03%

Size. Its share of the coin's supply right now.

20+

Location. Its place in the holder list, pools left out.
